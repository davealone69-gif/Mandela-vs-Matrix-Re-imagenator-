# Re-imaginator Rebrand — Internal Employee FAQ
**Version 1.0.0**  
**Classification: For Internal Use Only**

---

## 1. Why are we rebranding to the "Mandela vs Mandela vs Matrix Re-imaginator"?
Our company has transitioned from a basic software builder to an advanced, multi-agent cognitive simulation ecosystem. The new brand name **"Mandela vs Mandela vs Matrix Re-imaginator"** represents the core duality of our work:
*   **Mandela**: The study of human memory fluctuations, recollective anomalies, and collective timelines.
*   **Matrix**: The study of simulated digital grids, computational processing structures, and systemic information routing.
*   **Re-imaginator**: Our active role in designing tools that let users explore, create, and re-engineer alternate possibilities safely.

---

## 2. When does this change go into effect?
The official external launch is scheduled for **Week 12** of our launch timeline (refer to `project/12_week_timeline.md`). However, our internal systems transition, design tokens integration, and brand alignment workflows begin immediately.

---

## 3. What are my core communication rules under the new brand?
All employee communication (customer support, email correspondence, sales, and technical blogs) must align with our official brand voice:
*   **Confident**: Write with authority. Speak from empirical data and solid research. Avoid speculative phrases like "Maybe it's a glitch." Instead, write: "Our simulator computes specific divergence constants based on empirical memory logs."
*   **Warm**: Treat divergent memories and curious questions as fascinating facets of human nature. Never dismiss a customer's recollection as "incorrect" or "false memory." Instead, treat it as an active alternate timeline.
*   **Practical**: Give hands-on, direct utility. Cut through excessive theory and provide clear, actionable steps.

Yes. Effective immediately:

    > *"[Your Name] | [Your Title] | Mandela vs Mandela vs Matrix Re-imaginator"*
    > *"This email may contain confidential information. Mandela vs Mandela vs Matrix Re-imaginator is a trademark of [Company Name]."*
*   Ensure all slide decks use the color palettes and display fonts (Space Grotesk & Inter) specified in the `brand/brand_guidelines.md`.

## 6. How should I handle customer questions regarding the rebrand?
If customers, partners, or developers ask about the change, use these prepared guidelines:
*   **Customer**: "Is the app changing?"
    *   *Our response*: "Yes! We are upgrading our system to offer a far more robust, interactive, and beautiful simulation experience. Your existing workspaces remain fully secure and accessible."
*   **Developer**: "Are the APIs changing?"
    *   *Our response*: "We have introduced a new, comprehensive OpenAPI 3.0 specification (`api/openapi.yaml`) that provides RESTful endpoints to coordinate simulations securely. Backward-compatible endpoints will remain supported for 6 months."
