# Mandela vs Mandela vs Matrix Re-imaginator — Logo Usage & Asset Rules
**Version 1.0.0**  
**Classification: Brand Identity Standard**

---

## 1. Official Brand Logos
The **Mandela vs Mandela vs Matrix Re-imaginator** uses two main digital logos for consistent brand application. Both are created as high-fidelity SVG files to allow for infinite scaling without quality degradation.

*   **Primary Horizontal Logo (`assets/logo_primary.svg`)**:
    *   To be used in the top-left navigation headers, on corporate slides, landing pages, official newsletters, and documents.
    *   Combines the custom "shifting infinity glitch" brand mark with the wordmark "Mandela vs Mandela vs Matrix Re-imaginator".
*   **Brand Icon / Logo Mark (`assets/logo_icon.svg`)**:
    *   To be used as the app launcher icon, browser favicon, social media avatar, in-app loading spinners, or secondary footers.
    *   Displays the standalone "infinity glitch" emblem representing overlapping timelines and binary structures.

---

## 2. Spatial Margins & Isolation Zones
To ensure our logos always remain clear, legible, and visually commanding, a minimum "clear space" must be maintained around them on all four sides.

*   **Clear Space Rule**: The clear space is equal to **50% of the height** of the logo icon.
*   No other text, graphic elements, buttons, or margins may encroach inside this exclusion zone.

---

## 3. Recommended & Minimum Dimensions
*   **Primary Logo (Desktop)**:
    *   Recommended height in navigation: `36px` to `48px`.
    *   Minimum height: `28px` (digital display).
*   **Brand Icon (Desktop & Mobile)**:
    *   Recommended mobile app bar: `32px` by `32px`.
    *   Favicon size: `16px`, `32px`, or `192px` (PWA).
    *   Minimum size: `16px` by `16px`.

---

## 4. Logo Color Variations
To accommodate different environments, we maintain four official color variants:

1.  **Full Color (Default)**: Use only against dark backgrounds (`#070a13` / `#0d101b`). Displays the Matrix Green (`#10b981`) and Mandela Purple (`#8b5cf6`) glowing linear gradients.
2.  **Monochrome Light**: Entirely solid white (`#ffffff`). Used when printing on single-color dark backgrounds.
3.  **Monochrome Dark**: Entirely solid charcoal slate (`#070a13`). Used for light-theme backgrounds (such as plain white legal documents).
4.  **High-Contrast Green**: Solid green (`#10b981`). Used exclusively on terminal-style command-line outputs or low-power diagnostic indicators.

---

## 5. Strict Brand Prohibitions ("Do Not's")
To maintain the integrity of our visual assets, **DO NOT** perform any of the following alterations:

*   ❌ **Do not** change the color of the gradient to unapproved colors (e.g., orange, pink, or red).
*   ❌ **Do not** squeeze, stretch, or warp the logo's aspect ratio.
*   ❌ **Do not** apply drop shadows that are harsh, non-blurry, or unapproved.
*   ❌ **Do not** display the full-color logo against busy, low-contrast photographic backgrounds.
*   ❌ **Do not** rotate the logo or place it on an angle.
*   ❌ **Do not** alter the typography of the wordmark or replace it with a standard system font.
